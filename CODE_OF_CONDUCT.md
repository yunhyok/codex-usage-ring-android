# Code of Conduct

We want Usage Ring to be a constructive, technically rigorous project. Treat
people with respect, assume good faith while investigating disagreements, and
focus criticism on code, evidence, and decisions rather than individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
deliberate exposure of private information or credentials, and disruptive
conduct in issues, reviews, or project channels.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the channel described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled confidentially where
possible. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content and may restrict
participation when necessary to keep the project safe and useful.
